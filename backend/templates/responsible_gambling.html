⚠️ Warning Signs of Problem Gambling

Financial Indicators

  • Betting more than you can afford to lose
  • Borrowing money to gamble
  • Selling possessions to fund gambling
  • Unexplained debt or financial problems

Behavioral Changes

  • Lying about gambling activities
  • Neglecting work, family, or responsibilities
  • Chasing losses to win back money
  • Feeling irritable when trying to stop

Emotional & Mental Health

  • Using gambling to escape problems
  • Feeling guilt or shame about gambling
  • Anxiety or depression related to losses
  • Loss of control over gambling behavior

✅ Healthy Gambling Guidelines

💰

Set Strict Budget Limits

Never gamble with money you can't afford to lose. Set weekly/monthly limits and stick to them.

⏰

Time Management

Set time limits for gambling sessions. Take regular breaks and don't let gambling interfere with daily life.

🎯

Entertainment Mindset

View gambling as entertainment, not a way to make money. Expect to lose and treat wins as bonuses.

🚫

Never Chase Losses

Accept losses as part of gambling. Never try to win back money by continuing to gamble.
